KFX — Generic multi-asset intent clearing (cycle-based demo)

Participants declare swap intents (directed edges between assets with piecewise segments). The solver finds clearing cycles (length 2 or 3) and executes them greedily.

        Model summary
        
State
  - Participants i
  - Assets a
  - Net deltas Δ[i][a]

Clearing
  For each asset a:  Σ_i Δ[i][a] = 0

Intents
  For each participant, intents are swap edges:
    from asset X -> to asset Y
    segments define (capIn, rate, feeBps)

Execution primitive
  Execute a cycle (length 2 or 3):
    A->B, B->A   or   A->B, B->C, C->A
  This guarantees Σ_i Δ[i][a] = 0 for every asset a

Objective options
  A: maximize satisfaction for participants flagged focus=true
  B: maximize Σ (participantWeight * edgeFlowOut)
  C: B + small bias for higher-reputation participants being included in the cycle
