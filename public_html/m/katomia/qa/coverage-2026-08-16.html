1 · TXTRUTH-01 — never guess a FAILED verdict

BOARD-TXTRUTH1 (08-07): three false FAILURE verdicts on successful txs → user re-sent 0.01 USDC, real mainnet double spend — the only realized money harm of the period. Receipt socket has no reconnect; a drop between sig-save and receipt made the fallback guess FAILED. Fix 79858f4 ("confirm on chain, never guess") has zero regression protection.
Playwright fork of hbarxfer-auto-spike: sever the tx-receipt websocket between sign and receipt; assert the board still reaches SUCCESS sourced from chain confirmation, and FAILED is never shown for a mirror-confirmed tx. Absorbs the TXS-1/-3/-6 verdict-lifecycle cases (orphaned toasts, two-tab re-declare).
🪨 stone

2 · SIGNDEADTX-01 — refuse to sign an expired transaction

SIGN-DEADTX1 (08-07): Sign clicked 39s after window close → sig saved, success logged, mirror shows no consensus ever possible. The txValidity() gate's decoder matrix exists but is manual-only and doesn't test the gate.
Playwright on qa/wc-fixture.php (40s validity): wait out the window, tap Sign; assert refusal + expiry message, no signer:sign:saved success, no wallet round-trip. Also promote validdur-decode-matrix onto a cron.
🪨 stone

3 · ENRICHCOLD-01 — asset-enrich cold path stays fast, cached, parallel

ENRICH-COLDPATH1 (08-08→08-13, four defects/commits): never cached, session-lock serialised, 301 per call, one hung xproof blocking all assets; Kache.getData timeout deliberately deferred = known live hole. Recurring, confusion-prone subsystem (two enrich paths); nothing in QA touches it.
(a) backend script: enrich endpoint answers 200 not 301; cold < ~2.5s, warm < ~0.5s; two concurrent calls ≈ 1× not 2× (serialisation probe). (b) Playwright cold-profile board load: all chips enriched, no bare 0.0.x names, < 3s.
🪨 stone

4 · AMTSCALE-01 — settled amount is verified on the mirror

AMOUNT-SCALE1 (critical incident 07-07): display-unit leak → signed mainnet tx with 0-amount transfer. Guards shipped, but HBARXFER asserts a toast, never the on-chain amount — a scaling regression settling the wrong amount would pass green. Silent by construction.
(a) protocol script posting a display-unit offer for a decimals>0 token, asserting the backend FR/FS guards reject; (b) one-line hardening of hbarxfer-auto-spike: fetch the mirror transfer after the verdict, assert exactly 1,000,000 tinybar. Near-free on a harness already running 12×/day.
🪨 stone

5 · GOODWIN-FLOAT-01 — demo economy solvency, mirror-truthed

Three recurrences (GOODWIN-DEMO1/2/4; 18–19h public outages): payer deadlock → bot narrates rewards it cannot pay, logging SUCCESS throughout. Only detectable by chain cross-check, which nothing automates. Memory rule: verify on the mirror transfer list, never the SUCCESS log.
Daily backend script: mirror balance check on the Goodwin payer + treat-float 0.0.9862254 vs precheck threshold; take the last narrated reward round from the bot log and assert matching legs on the mirror transfer list. Red = run kbot/goodwin-topup.cjs --go.
🪨 stone

Pair kWallet with two testnet accounts, wrong one active, tap Sign: assert abort toast + board:sign:account-mismatch kdiag, no signTx reaches the wallet.

Zero-cost adds alongside any of the above: WCSINGLEINIT-01, PRIVJOIN-01 (sand-grade).

Shape of coverage

The continuous suite is concentrated on the WalletConnect session lifecycle and the statsync protocol — where it is genuinely excellent and discriminating — plus two e2e canaries that catch gross outages within hours. What it does not watch is whether the app tells the truth about money: verdict provenance, expiry refusal, settled amounts, and demo-economy solvency all regress silently today. On the body diagram: hands and memory are well-tested, the gut (enrich) and the truth-telling nervous system have no cells at all.
