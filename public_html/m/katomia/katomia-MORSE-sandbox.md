# **MORSE Sandbox: Applied Operational Research on Next-Gen Financial Systems**

## **Overview**

We are exploring a collaboration between MORSE students/researchers and a live, emerging financial platform (**Katomia**) built on Hedera Hashgraph.

Katomia is a **multi-asset, multi-party transaction engine** designed to enable complex exchanges beyond traditional pairwise trading. Instead of simple trades (A ↔ B), it supports **arbitrary baskets of assets across multiple participants**, creating a rich environment of **networked optimisation problems**.

This creates a unique opportunity:  
👉 apply **operational research, statistics, econometrics, and game theory** to a *live system* at the intersection of **Web3, tokenisation, and intelligent financial infrastructure**.

Hedera’s **high throughput, low latency, and fast finality** make it particularly suited to experimenting with **real-time vs global optimisation**, allowing research to move beyond theory into **practical, testable systems**.

---

## **Why this matters**

- Financial systems are evolving toward **tokenised, programmable markets**

- Matching and settlement are becoming **multi-dimensional optimisation problems**

- There is a growing need for **intelligent coordination layers** on top of distributed ledgers

Katomia acts as a **sandbox for this new paradigm** — where MORSE techniques can directly influence how future markets operate.

---

## **Core Research Themes & Experiments**

### **1. Optimisation & Network Flow (Operational Research)**

- Compare **greedy matching vs global optimisation (ILP / flow models)**

- Model trades as **multi-asset networks with cycles**

- Evaluate:
  
  - total value cleared
  
  - efficiency vs speed
  
  - impact of constraints (indivisibility, limits)

---

### **2. Market Behaviour & Statistical Modelling**

- Model **liquidity and fill probability**

- Analyse:
  
  - order size distributions
  
  - time-to-fill dynamics

- Identify inefficiencies between **theoretical vs actual clearing**

---

### **3. Econometrics & Implied Pricing**

- Extract **implicit exchange rates** from multi-asset trades

- Build **time-series models** of emergent pricing

- Detect **arbitrage loops** and deviations from equilibrium

---

### **4. Game Theory & Strategy**

- Study **strategic order placement**

- Explore:
  
  - order splitting vs single orders
  
  - information asymmetry
  
  - incentive design (fees, rewards)

- Observe how **equilibria emerge in multi-party systems**

---

### **5. Distributed Coordination (Hedera Layer)**

- Analyse **lock → sign → submit** as a coordination mechanism

- Study:
  
  - latency vs optimality trade-offs
  
  - participant behaviour under delayed consensus

- Compare **off-chain optimisation vs on-chain final outcomes**

---

## **Example Flagship Experiment**

**“Real-Time Matching vs Batch Clearing”**

- Immediate (greedy) execution vs delayed global optimisation

- Measure:
  
  - efficiency gains
  
  - fairness
  
  - system stability

This mirrors real-world exchange design and is directly testable.
